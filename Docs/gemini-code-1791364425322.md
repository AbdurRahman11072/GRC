# SaaS Audit Platform: System Blueprint & Database Schema

## Part 1: The Complete 4-Team Audit Workflow

### 1. Onboarding & Organization Setup
*   **User Registration:** A user logs in and establishes their firm as an **Organization**.
*   **Member Management:** The Organization Super Admin invites staff members to join the platform.
*   **Knowledge Base Creation:** The Admin defines **Guideline Categories** and populates them with specific **Guidelines**. This acts as the organization’s standardized rulebook.
*   **Monetization Gate:** The Admin purchases a platform subscription, unlocking the ability to create active Audit Projects.

### 2. Project Creation & Team Assembly
*   **Project Initiation:** The Admin creates a new Audit Project, defining scope, dates, and contracts.
*   **The Four-Team Structure:** The Admin assigns members to the project, strictly divided into four distinct groups:
    *   **Org Team 1 & Org Team 2:** The auditing firm's internal groups.
    *   **Client Team 1 & Client Team 2:** The auditee's internal groups.
    *   *Workflow alignment:* Org Team 1 maps directly to Client Team 1, and Org Team 2 maps to Client Team 2.

### 3. ToR Distribution & The DRT Trigger
*   **ToR Mapping:** The Admin creates specific Terms of Reference (ToRs), structuring them hierarchically (Parent/Child).
*   **Assignment & Files:** Each ToR is assigned to the relevant team members, and instructional files are attached.
*   **Drafting & Publishing:** ToRs are kept in a "Draft" state. Once the Admin clicks **Publish**, the project goes live.
*   **Automated Dispatch:** The system instantly fires email notifications to all members of the four teams, officially opening the Data Request Tracking (DRT) phase.

### 4. Data Collection & Live Calculation
*   **Collaborative DRT:** Client teams receive the requests, gather the required evidence, and submit it directly through the system.
*   **Automated Weighting Engine:** When an Org team member marks a DRT submission as verified/done, the system instantly calculates the specific weight and score for that ToR.
*   **Universal Visibility:** A centralized dashboard updates in real-time, allowing all assigned members to see the live progress and calculated weights.

## Part 2: Database Architecture & Fields

### 1. The Identity & Access Layer

**`users`**
*   `id` (string, pk)
*   `name` (string)
*   `email` (string)
*   `phone` (string)
*   `nid` (string)
*   `address` (string)
*   `role` (string)
*   `designation` (string)
*   `joinig_date` (Date)
*   `createdAt` (timestamp)
*   `updateAt` (timestamp)

**`Organizations`**
*   `id` (string, pk)
*   `org_Name` (string)
*   `org_Type` (string)
*   `org_Address` (string)
*   `org_Phone` (string)
*   `org_email` (string)
*   `org_website` (string)
*   `org_linkedIn` (string)
*   `org_facebook` (string)
*   `numberOfEmployee` (string)
*   `createdBy` (string, fk -> users.id)
*   `createdAt` (timestamp)
*   `updateAt` (timestamp)

**`OrganizationMember`** (Junction Table)
*   `id` (string, pk)
*   `org_id` (string, fk -> Organizations.id)
*   `user_id` (string, fk -> users.id)
*   `org_role` (string)
*   `createdAt` (timestamp)
*   `updateAt` (timestamp)

*   `address` (string)
*   `website` (status)
*   `phone` (string)
*   `createdAt` (timestamp)
*   `updateAt` (timestamp)
